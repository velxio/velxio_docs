Click **Add** in the toolbar (or the **+** button on the canvas) to open
the component picker:

![The component picker](../../../assets/docs/circuit-editor/component-picker.png)

- **Search** by name or function: `oled`, `dht`, `resistor`, `servo`…
- **Filter** by category with the dropdown on the right — boards, displays,
  sensors, motors, logic, analog parts, and branded modules.
- Cards with a **PRO badge** need a paid plan to _run_ — you can see the
  whole catalog regardless. See [plans](/docs/getting-started/plans/).

Click a card and the part lands on the canvas. From there:

- **Drag** it to position it.
- **Click** to select (a dashed marquee shows the selection).
- **Right-click** for the [part inspector](/docs/circuit-editor/part-inspector/) —
  properties, pinout, datasheet, rotate and delete.
- **Delete** or **Backspace** removes the selected part.

Every part on the canvas is live during simulation: LEDs light, displays
draw, buttons click, sensors report the values you set on them.

The full catalog, with a page per part, is in the
[parts reference](/docs/parts/overview/).
