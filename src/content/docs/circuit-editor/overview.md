The circuit editor is the canvas where your project lives. From here you:

- **Add parts** from the component picker, organized by category and brand.
- **Wire pins together** by clicking a pin and dragging to another.
- **Edit properties** of each part — a resistor's value, an LED's color, an
  I2C address — from the properties panel.
- **Read datasheets** by right-clicking any component: specs, pinout and a
  link to buy the real part.

The pages in this section cover each of those in detail, plus the full
keyboard shortcut list.
