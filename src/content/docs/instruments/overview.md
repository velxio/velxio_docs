Velxio ships virtual bench instruments so you can *see* what your circuit is
doing, not just guess:

- **Oscilloscope** — connect its probes to any signal and watch it live:
  PWM duty cycles, sensor outputs, communication lines.
- **Meters and other instruments** — measure voltages and currents at any
  point of the circuit.
- **Analog simulation** — behind the scenes, analog parts are solved by a
  SPICE-class engine (diodes, transistors, op-amps), so what you measure
  behaves like the real thing.

Start with the [oscilloscope guide](/docs/instruments/oscilloscope/).
