Velxio is an online electronics simulator. You place real boards and
components on a canvas, wire them together, write the program in the built-in
code editor, and press **Play** — your code compiles and runs on an emulated
board, driving the components exactly like the real hardware would.

## What you can simulate

- **Boards**: Arduino (UNO, Nano, Mega), the ESP32 family (classic, S3, C3
  and more), Raspberry Pi Pico and Pico W, STM32 (Blue Pill, Black Pill,
  Discovery…), full Linux Raspberry Pi (Zero through 5) and ATtiny85.
- **Components**: LEDs, displays (OLED, TFT, LCD, e-paper), sensors, motors,
  logic ICs, analog parts and branded modules from DFRobot, Seeed, M5Stack
  and Grove.
- **Languages**: Arduino C++, MicroPython and ESP-IDF, depending on the
  board.
- **Instruments**: serial monitor, oscilloscope and more, to see what your
  circuit is really doing.
