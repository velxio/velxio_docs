Velxio has a built-in AI assistant that understands your project — the
circuit on the canvas *and* the code in the editor. It works in three modes:

- **Basic** — a chat that answers questions with your project as context:
  "why doesn't my LED turn on?", "what does this compiler error mean?"
- **Agent** — the assistant acts on the project for you: it can add and
  wire components, and write or fix code. You watch what it does and stay
  in control.
- **Tutor** — a guided electronics teacher: it proposes exercises, checks
  your circuit and explains the theory as you go.

The AI assistant is available on paid plans, with usage quotas per plan —
see [plans](/docs/getting-started/plans/).
