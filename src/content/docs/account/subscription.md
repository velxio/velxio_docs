## Subscribing

Pick a plan on the [pricing page](https://velxio.dev/pricing) — **Maker**
or **Pro**, billed monthly. Checkout supports card (Stripe) and PayPal.
The features unlock on your account immediately after payment; see
[what each plan includes](/docs/getting-started/plans/).

## Managing your plan

From the **Account** menu you can reach your subscription management
portal: change the payment method, download invoices, upgrade, or cancel.

- **Cancelling** stops future renewals — the plan stays active until the
  end of the paid period.
- After it lapses, your account reverts to the free tier. Your projects
  are never deleted; pro-only boards in old projects simply won't run
  until you re-subscribe.

## Quotas

The AI assistant's **cycles** counter (bottom of the chat panel) shows
your remaining daily and monthly allowance and when the daily quota
resets. Compiles and simulation on open boards remain available on every
tier.

## Schools and teams

Using Velxio for a classroom? There's a dedicated
[For schools](https://velxio.dev/for-schools) track — reach out through
it for class-sized setups.
