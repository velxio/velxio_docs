You can open Velxio and simulate without an account. Signing up (email +
verification) adds the things that need to persist:

- **Saved projects** — your work, available from any device.
- **Public project links** — share a circuit with one URL.
- **Plan features** — a paid subscription attaches to your account; see
  [plans](/docs/getting-started/plans/).

## Signing in

Use **Sign in** on the site or the **Account** menu in the editor. Your
avatar appears at the bottom-left of the editor when you're signed in.

## Interface language

Velxio's interface is available in multiple languages — English, Spanish,
Portuguese, Italian, French, Chinese, German, Japanese and Russian. Switch
with the language selector (globe icon) in the site header; the editor
follows.

## Signing out and account data

Sign out from the **Account** menu. Your saved projects stay in your
account; public links keep working while the project remains public.
