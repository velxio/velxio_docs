You can use Velxio without an account, but signing in lets you save
projects, sync them across devices and unlock paid features.

In this section:

- **Account** — sign up, sign in, profile and interface language.
- **Subscription** — subscribe, manage or cancel your plan, invoices.
- **Troubleshooting** — what to check when the simulation won't start,
  compilation is slow, or web flash doesn't detect your board.
