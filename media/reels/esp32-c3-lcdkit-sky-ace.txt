INSTAGRAM
Sky Ace is a full jet shooter running on the ESP32-C3-LCDkit and nothing else. Turn the knob to steer, push it to fire, and hit REMOTE for a mega bomb through the IR receiver. The round GC9A01 shows waves of fighters, gunships that drop power-ups and a flying fortress, all drawn from a sprite on an FPU-less chip.

Try it free at velxio.dev (link in bio)

Title: Sky Ace: a jet shooter on the ESP32-C3 round LCD #shorts

A complete arcade shooter on the ESP32-C3-LCDkit: turn the knob to fly, push to fire, and the IR remote drops a mega bomb. Everything is drawn into a 240x240 sprite and pushed to the round GC9A01. Open it in your browser: https://velxio.dev/example/esp32-c3-lcdkit-sky-ace
