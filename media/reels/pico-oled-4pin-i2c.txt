Title: Pico + SSD1306 OLED counter in 4 wires #shorts

A Raspberry Pi Pico talks to a 4-pin SSD1306 OLED over I2C and shows a live counter. The sketch uses Adafruit GFX and SSD1306 libraries on SDA GP4 and SCL GP5. Open it in your browser: https://velxio.dev/example/pico-oled-4pin-i2c
