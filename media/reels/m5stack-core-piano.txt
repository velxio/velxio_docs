The M5Stack Core turns its three front buttons into a tiny piano. Hold A, B or C and the matching key lights orange on the LCD while the speaker plays C4, E4 or G4. No wiring, just the board and M5Unified.

Try it free at velxio.dev (link in bio)

Title: M5Stack Core button piano: three keys on one board #shorts

The three front buttons of the M5Stack Core play C4, E4 and G4 on the built-in speaker, and the LCD lights the key you are holding. It runs entirely on the board with M5Unified, no extra parts. Open it in your browser: https://velxio.dev/example/m5stack-core-piano
