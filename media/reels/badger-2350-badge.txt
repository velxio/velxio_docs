The Pimoroni Badger 2350 boots into an e-paper badge on a 264x176 SSD1680 panel. Button A shows the badge, B reads the PCF85063A clock, C shows battery and USB info. UP and DOWN switch the rear case lights on and off. It all runs on an RP2350 emulator in your browser.

Try it free at velxio.dev (link in bio)
