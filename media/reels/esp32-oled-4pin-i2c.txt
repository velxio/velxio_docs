Title: ESP32 SSD1306 OLED counter in the browser #shorts

An ESP32 drives a 4-pin SSD1306 OLED over I2C and prints a counter that updates every 500 ms. The whole circuit runs in Velxio, a free browser simulator. Open it in your browser: https://velxio.dev/example/esp32-oled-4pin-i2c
