INSTAGRAM
Brick Blaster runs entirely on the M5 Cardputer ADV, no wiring and no microSD. The 240x135 LCD draws through an off-screen sprite so nothing tears, and the 56-key keyboard steers the paddle. Each brick row is a note of a pentatonic scale, so clearing rows walks up the scale and you can hear your progress.

Try it free at velxio.dev (link in bio)
