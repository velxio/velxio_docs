## About this board

The 128 KB Blue Pill. Same Cortex-M3, same pinout and same peripherals as the
[F103C8](/docs/boards/reference/stm32-bluepill/) — twice the flash.

Many boards sold as "Blue Pill" carry the CB die, which is why both are here:
the flash size is modeled, so a build that fits on one and not the other tells
you the truth about your hardware.
