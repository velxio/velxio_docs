## About this board

The RP2350 in the XIAO footprint: dual core at 150 MHz, 2 MB flash. It runs on
the same in-browser RP2350 engine as the Pimoroni boards, in Arduino C++ or
MicroPython.

## Start here

- [Blink](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-rp2350-blink) — the shortest check
  that the board and toolchain work.
- [OLED over I2C](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-rp2350-oled) — an SSD1306 on
  the header pins.
- [Knob and button](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-rp2350-knob-button) —
  analog in, digital in, an LED out.
