## About this board

The STM32F411CE Black Pill: Cortex-M4 with an FPU, noticeably more headroom
than a Blue Pill and the usual next step up.

Its sibling the [F401CE](/docs/boards/reference/stm32-blackpill-f401/) shares
the footprint but not the flash or clock ceiling, so confirm the marking on
your own board before assuming a sketch ports across.
