## About this board

DFRobot's small two-wheel robot for the micro:bit (ROB0148-EN), with the
[BBC micro:bit V2](/docs/boards/reference/microbit-v2/) standing in its edge socket. The micro:bit is the computer; the robot
adds two motors behind a driver on I2C, two red headlights, two line sensors
under the nose, four RGB LEDs, an IR receiver, a buzzer, an ultrasonic
socket and two servo ports.

On the canvas the robot is one board: place it and the micro:bit comes
seated. Click a line sensor's indicator to put that sensor over black, or
open a 3D World and let the floor set it while the robot drives.

**MicroPython only** for now: no Arduino/C++ and no MakeCode blocks. Radio
between boards on the canvas and Bluetooth are not modelled.

## Start here

- [Drive, turn and blink the headlights](https://velxio.dev/example/microbit-v2-maqueen-lite-drive):
  the motors and the headlights, with the `maqueen.py` driver beside
  `main.py`.
- [Line follower](https://velxio.dev/example/microbit-v2-maqueen-lite-line):
  the classic first robot program, on the two sensors at P13 and P14.
