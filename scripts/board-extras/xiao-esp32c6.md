## About this board

Seeed's WiFi-6 capable RISC-V C6 in the XIAO footprint. Beyond the board
itself, it is the one that carries the **Grove and ReSpeaker ecosystem** in the
catalog: the gallery has gesture sensors over I2C and SPI, and a ReSpeaker Lite
with far-field microphone, speaker output and direction-of-arrival tracking,
all built on this XIAO.

## Start here

- [Blink](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-esp32c6-blink) — the shortest check
  that the board and toolchain work.
- [Grove Smart IR Gesture](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-c6-grove-gesture) —
  a XIAO dropped straight onto a Grove socket, no wires.
- [ReSpeaker Lite: track the talker](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-c6-respeaker-doa)
  — the most complete of the audio projects.
