## About this board

ST's own STM32F407VG evaluation board — Cortex-M4 with FPU and a far wider pin
map than the pill boards, which is the reason to choose it: the F407 brings out
peripherals the 48-pin boards never expose.

It is the largest STM32 in the catalog by pin count, so it is also the one to
place when a project has simply run out of pins elsewhere.
