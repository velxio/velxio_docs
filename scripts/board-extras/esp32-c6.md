## About this board

Espressif's ESP32-C6 DevKit — the RISC-V, WiFi-6 generation — with the same
language trio as the rest of the ESP32 family: Arduino C++, MicroPython and
ESP-IDF, switched from the toolbar.

From a sketch's point of view it behaves like the other RISC-V ESP32s in the
catalog; the C6 is here so projects targeting the newer radio can be prototyped
on the right part.

## Start here

- [Blink LED](https://velxio.dev/example/c6-blink) — the starter.
- [SSD1306 OLED over I2C](https://velxio.dev/example/c6-oled-i2c) — the usual
  next step, and a check that the I2C bus behaves.
