## About this board

The STM32F103C8 Blue Pill: Cortex-M3 with 64 KB of flash, the cheapest way
into ARM and the board most STM32 tutorials assume.

Pick the variant you actually own. The **F103C8 has 64 KB**, the
[F103CB](/docs/boards/reference/stm32-bluepill-f103cb/) has 128 KB, and that
difference is modeled here — a sketch that overflows on this page's board is
the same sketch that would overflow on your desk.
