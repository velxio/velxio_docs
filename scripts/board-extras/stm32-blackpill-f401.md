## About this board

The STM32F401CE Black Pill — Cortex-M4 with FPU, the smaller of the two Black
Pills in the catalog.

Everything written for the [F411CE](/docs/boards/reference/stm32-blackpill/)
applies at the API level; what differs is memory and maximum clock, both of
which are modeled here rather than assumed.
