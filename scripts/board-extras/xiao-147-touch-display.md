## About this board

The flagship of the three: a JD9853A 172 x 320 IPS panel with a capacitive
touch layer and a microSD slot, on a carrier with a pre-soldered XIAO nRF52840
Plus, two user buttons (USR1 D19, USR2 D15), a 6-axis IMU and a battery
connector. The canvas shows the board front and back. A click on the glass is
a finger on the touch controller; the card holds your project's files plus
whatever you add through the board's SD Card panel.

Drive the panel with **Seeed_GFX2**, which the board declares for you. Tilt
the board and set the battery level from **Sensors** on the canvas toolbar.
The PDM microphone is not modelled.
