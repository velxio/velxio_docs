## About this board

The Renesas RA4M1 — the same MCU as the Arduino UNO R4 Minima — in the XIAO
footprint, with a 12-bit DAC on A0. The in-browser engine boots the firmware
Seeed's Renesas core produces; `Serial` is the USB CDC port and `Serial1` sits
on D6/D7.

## Start here

- [Blink](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-ra4m1-blink) — the shortest check
  that the board and toolchain work.
- [OLED over I2C](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-ra4m1-oled) — an SSD1306 on
  the header pins.
- [Knob and button](https://velxio.dev/example/xiao-ra4m1-knob-button) —
  analog in, digital in, an LED out.
