## About this board

The Netduino 2 — an STM32F205 (Cortex-M3) in the same Arduino-shaped outline as
the [Netduino Plus 2](/docs/boards/reference/stm32-netduino-plus2/), one step
down in core and memory.

As with its sibling, it runs here as a plain STM32 under the STM32duino core.

## Start here

[Serial output](https://velxio.dev/example/stm32-netduino2-serial) is this
board's own example — the shortest confirmation that it builds, boots and
reaches the serial monitor. STM32 boards need a **paid plan** — see
[plans](/docs/getting-started/plans/).
