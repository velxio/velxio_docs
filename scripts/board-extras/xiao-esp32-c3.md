## About this board

Seeed's tiny C3 — the same single-core RISC-V chip as the
[C3 DevKit](/docs/boards/reference/esp32-c3/), in the XIAO footprint with a
much smaller pin set. Sketches port straight across as long as the GPIO
numbers they use exist here; the map below is the authority.

## Start here

No separate starter: open
[New ESP32-C3 project](https://velxio.dev/example/c3-blink) and swap the board,
or place it from **Add Component**.
